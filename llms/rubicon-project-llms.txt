## Terms
- [Yield Optimization](https://www.magnite.com/term/yield-optimization/): The process of maximizing revenue from ad inventory by adjusting pricing strategies and inventory allocation\.
- [Yield Management](https://www.magnite.com/term/yield-management/): Strategies used by publishers to maximize revenue from their ad inventory by adjusting prices and inventory allocation\.
- [Whitelist](https://www.magnite.com/term/whitelist/): A list of approved websites or apps where an advertiser permits their ads to be displayed\.
- [Video on Demand \(VOD\)](https://www.magnite.com/term/video-on-demand-vod/): A system that enables users to stream or download video content whenever they choose rather than at a scheduled broadcast time\.
- [Video Completion Rate \(VCR\)](https://www.magnite.com/term/video-completion-rate-vcr/): The percentage of video ads that were played in their entirety compared to the number of times they were started\.
