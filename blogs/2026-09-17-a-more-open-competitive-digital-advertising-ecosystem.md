The remedies decision in the Google ad tech case is an important moment for digital advertising. Google’s dominance of open-web display advertising has stifled growth, innovation, and investment in the industry for more than a decade. We welcome the court’s focus on competition, fairness and greater choice for publishers and advertisers.
